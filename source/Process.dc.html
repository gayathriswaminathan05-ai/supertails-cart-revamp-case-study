Appendix

The evidence behind the decisions.

Documented inputs are credible design evidence. They are not a substitute for participant research, and nothing here is presented as research findings.

A · Proto-personas

Two shopping modes. One adaptable cart.

Evidence-informed · not validated segments

Persona 01

The Busy Repeat Buyer

Context
Reorders familiar pet essentials, sometimes on short notice.
Motivation
Keep supplies predictable with minimal effort.
Behavior
Scans EDD. Expects saved address and payment to remain useful.
Friction
Split deliveries and repeated checkout actions.
Expectation
Quickly understand arrival and continue with confidence.

Persona 02

The Value-Seeking Planner

Context
Reviews price and available offers before committing.
Motivation
Know the final amount is justified.
Behavior
Compares coupon benefits and checks the summary.
Friction
Ambiguous applied states or unexplained price changes.
Expectation
See what is saved, why, and what remains payable.

Cross-cutting need: either persona may buy pharmacy products and need a clear prescription recovery path.

B · Competitive learnings

Borrow principles, not screens.

Historical references · not a current audit
References
Pattern / strength
Transfer limitation
Learning for Supertails
Blinkit · Zepto · Instamart
Prominent EDD and speed cues support rapid scanning.
Speed messaging must reflect local availability and fulfillment.
Standardize NOW / FAST / EXPRESS / STANDARD.
Amazon · Amazon Now · Flipkart
Split-delivery models and saved checkout states explain complexity.
A shared date does not imply a shared shipment.
Preserve delivery identity even when EDD matches.
Zomato · Swiggy
Explicit coupon benefit and applied-state feedback make savings legible.
Promotional emphasis can obscure the final payable amount.
Show monetary impact beside the coupon state.
Myntra · Nykaa
Compact product hierarchy keeps price and key attributes scannable.
Density must preserve quantity and removal affordances.
Reduce product-tile weight; keep usable controls.

Limitations are design trade-offs for Supertails, not verified competitor defects. Also taken forward: pet-commerce-specific delivery and product information.

C · Primary research

A validation plan, ready to run.

Proposed · not yet run · no results claimed

Participant mix

5–8 moderated sessions

New and returning shoppers, repeat essentials buyers, coupon-focused shoppers and pharmacy purchasers.

Research goal

Explain it without help

Can shoppers explain their deliveries, savings and next checkout action without assistance?

Session approach

15–20 minutes each

Realistic matched carts. Counterbalance old and new order where practical. Record consented task behavior.

Recovery success; mistaken delivery assumptions.
Select payment and continue

Time, redundant taps, errors and confidence.
